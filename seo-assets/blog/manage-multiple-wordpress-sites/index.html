Manage multiple WordPress sites (without losing your mind)

Field notes from the Godseye team · 5 min read Guide

If you run client sites — an agency, a freelancer, a value-added reseller — you don't have a WordPress problem. You have a many-websites problem. Logging into site 12 to change an image, remembering which client is on which stack, fielding the same "is my site okay?" calls a dozen times a week. That overhead is real revenue you're leaving on the table.

The chaos tax of running many sites

  • Context switching — each wp-admin login is a small reset of your attention.
  • Forgotten logins — a password manager full of client credentials you touch once a month.
  • Security anxiety — wondering whether site #7 got its plugin update, and whether it even matters.
  • Client requests — "can you just update the banner?" — each one a separate queue.

Multiply that by a handful of sites and it stops being management; it becomes babysitting.

One chat for the whole portfolio

The alternative is to run every site from a single conversation, switching sites the way you'd switch a channel:

「 on the bakery site, update the price on the wedding page — then run a security check on all sites 」

That's the whole workflow. Point a request at a site, handle it in plain English, and move on. No login server, no dashboard tour, no digging through one client's admin to find where the coupon settings went.

What stays true whether you have 1 site or 40

  • You approve everything. Nothing ships to a client's live site without your OK.
  • Comments and analytics, summarized. "What's the engagement like this month?" — an answer, not a slog through analytics tabs.
  • Recurring maintenance. Weekly health checks and updates can just run, so "is the site okay?" has a real answer.

Run more sites, not more tabs

Client work scales with your ability to manage more of it without more chaos. When every site lives in one chat, adding a client stops adding a dashboard to your day. That's how a portfolio of sites stops being a job and starts being a business.
