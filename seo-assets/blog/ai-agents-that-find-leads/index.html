AI agents that find leads while you sleep

Field notes from the Godseye team · 5 min read Guide

You can't grow a business if finding customers depends entirely on you being awake, available, and typing. An AI agent changes that. It finds you leads around the clock, so your pipeline fills even while you close your laptop.

Leads are the one thing you can't pause

Revenue follows conversations, and conversations follow outreach. But prospecting is the easiest task to put off. It's repetitive, it's awkward, and there's always something louder demanding your attention. An agent doesn't procrastinate. Give it a target customer and it goes to work.

What an agent does, step by step

  • Finds. Searches the web for people who need exactly what you sell.
  • Qualifies. Checks they're a real fit, not just a name on a list.
  • Reaches out. Sends a first message in a natural tone, not spam.
  • Follows up. Keeps the thread alive so nothing goes cold.

"Every day, 24/7" is the whole point

You might find leads a few hours a week. An agent does it in the background, all day, every day, 365 days a year. It's the difference between a pipeline that depends on your energy and one that runs on its own.

You stay in control

This isn't a robot spamming strangers without you. The agent reports back and waits for your approval before it goes further. You steer. It executes. You approve ideas, it does the moving. The leads come to you, filtered and ready, and you decide what happens next.
