## THE HARD RULES (from the boss — never break these)

### Language
- 🚫 NO jargon. "communicate with your agent on Telegram", NOT "text". "No-code developer friendly" framing, natural.
- 🚫 NO arrows/emoji spam/bullet spam in customer-facing copy.
- 🚫 NO "what's coming" — the product is **COMPLETE**. Show what it DOES.
- 🚫 NO build % / "we're working on X" — show the working product.
- ✅ "Self-learning — grows with you."
- ✅ Outcome over feature: "Your site, handled." not "WooCommerce + analytics integration."

### Length guardrails (verify everything)
- Tweets ≤ **280 chars** (verify with a counter).
- Bios ≤ **160 chars**.
- Headlines ≤ **15 words**.
- One CTA per section.

### Structure
- Short sentences. Lead with the benefit.
- Human-likability = the moat. Godseye feels like a person who runs your site, not software. Lean into that.
- 'The Architect' persona — the agent is your site's caretaker, proactive, reliable.

## CORE MESSAGING PILLARS

1. **Effortless control** — "Run your whole WordPress site by chatting on Telegram."
2. **No dev required** — "No dev hire. No code. Just talk."
3. **Peace of mind** — "Your site never breaks while you sleep."
4. **Self-learning** — "The agent learns how YOU like things done. It grows with you."
5. **Human feel** — "It feels like a real person managing your site."
