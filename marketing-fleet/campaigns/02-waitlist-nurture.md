# Segmentation Note

- **All waitlist subscribers** receive Email 1 immediately on capture — it's the launch open and pushes the discount while it's live.
- **Engaged segment** (clicked Email 1 or 2): continue through the full Email 2→5 sequence.
- **Cold segment** (no open/click on Email 1 after ~5 days, never touched the link): typically shorter path — Email 3 (founder), then Email 5 (urgency), or Email 5 with an "are you still in?" line; can suppress Emails 2/4 to avoid spam fatigue.
- **Pull all sends** once the founder-pricing window closes; swap Email 5 for a plain "prices updated" note if expiry isn't honored.
- **Geo:** all copy targets US/UK/CA/AU/DE/FR English-speaking audiences; US day-timing for send windows.
