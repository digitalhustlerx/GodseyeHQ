Template 01
Welcome New Customer
First impression that turns a buyer into a fan. Warm, personal, sets expectations.
"Hey [Name]! Welcome aboard. I'm [Business] and I'm thrilled you're here. Here's what happens next: [1-sentence流程]. Any questions? Just reply — I'm here."
Template 02
Follow Up on Inquiry
The follow-up that closes. Not pushy — just helpful.
"Hey [Name], just checking in on [topic]. Did you get a chance to look at it? Happy to answer any questions or help you get started."
Template 03
Request a Review
Ask for the testimonial at the right moment — when they're happy.
"Hey [Name]! So glad you loved [result]. Would you mind sharing a quick review? It helps others find us. Just reply with a few words and I'll take care of the rest."
Template 04
Handle a Complaint
Turn a negative into loyalty. Acknowledge, fix, follow up.
"Hey [Name], I hear you and I'm sorry about [issue]. Here's what I'm doing to fix it: [action]. I'll follow up in [timeframe] to make sure it's right."
Template 05
Upsell / Cross-sell
Natural upsell that feels like a recommendation, not a sales pitch.
"Hey [Name]! Since you loved [product], you might like [related]. A lot of customers use them together. Want me to set you up?"
