# GodsEye Agents School — Launch Pack

## Positioning

GodsEye Agents School is a practical community and forward-deployment service for people who want to use AI agents in real work. It is not a generic AI course, a chatbot, or a promise of autonomous perfection.

The core promise:

> We help you choose, configure, understand, and safely use an agent environment for your work.

- VPS and harness configuration
- One practical workflow

- Plain-English guide

Scope: one customer, one environment, one primary workflow. Additional integrations are quoted separately.

### Private AI Network — from $2,500
For teams, agencies, and creators needing multiple agents, workspaces, integrations, and operating rules.

Updates, troubleshooting, and monthly operating review.
