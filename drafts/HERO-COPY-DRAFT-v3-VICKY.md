## WHAT VICKY TAUGHT US (the 5 rules)

1. **She doesn't have a website.** Stop assuming people do. "Your WordPress site" means nothing to her. → Lead with what she HAS (a phone, Telegram, a business).

2. **She was drawn to "conversing" and "building whatever you want."** That's the closest the current copy gets to human language. → Make THAT the headline energy.

3. **She liked the design but couldn't understand the product.** Pretty doesn't sell if the words don't land. → Simplify every sentence until a non-technical person nods.

4. **She wanted to "move it to Telegram."** The channel IS the product for her. She doesn't want a website — she wants something living where she already is. → Telegram isn't a feature. It's the whole pitch for normies.

5. **She's overwhelmed.** "I don't know what to look out for." The site needs to reduce overwhelm, not add to it. → One clear message: "this handles things so you don't have to."
