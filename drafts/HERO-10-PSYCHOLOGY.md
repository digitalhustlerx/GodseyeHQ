It can be everything you want it to be, all at the same time. Hiring one person costs thousands. This costs $9. And it never sleeps, never complains, and works 365 days a year. ## MY PICK FOR THE 5 SLIDES

If I had to pick 5 that cover the widest psychological ground:

1. **Option 7 (Simplicity)** — reduces fear first. "It's just a chat." If they don't understand, nothing else matters.
2. **Option 5 (Trust/Relief)** — hits the exhaustion. "You're tired. Here's help."
3. **Option 1 (Greed)** — the rational justification. "Less than a phone bill."
4. **Option 8 (Power)** — the depth reveal. "Spawn agents. Command a team."
5. **Option 10 (Belonging)** — the closer. "Regular people do this. Join them."

This sequence: understand → feel → justify → discover depth → belong.
