## 1. THE RECOMMENDED BEST ANGLE (for a broad, all-around audience)

**Lead with the whole-business agent, not the WordPress tool.** Godseye's strongest, most broadly-appealing message is: *your business, run by an AI agent that lives in the chat you already use — it takes the work off your plate, thinks ahead, and keeps you in control.* This is the only framing that survives contact with every draft's best material. It does not require the visitor to have a WordPress site, to be technical, or to understand "AI" as a category. It sells a feeling and an outcome, not software: the relief of having a reliable living entity handling the chaos while you stay the boss.

The single most persuasive *bundled* message pulls from four proven threads and weaves them into one promise:

> **It's a living agent that runs your business from Telegram. You tell it once. It handles the rest. It thinks ahead while you're not looking. And you approve everything it ships, so you're never out of control.**

This works because it is simultaneously *simple* (it's just a chat — from the PSYCHOLOGY and simplify drafts), *relieving* ("you're tired of doing everything yourself. Here's someone who never gets tired"), *proactive* ("it doesn't just answer. It thinks ahead" — the strongest **differentiator** the drafts agree on), *safe* ("you stay in control" — the safety-by-design thread from the live waitlist), and *broad* (a whole business, not a website). The WordPress management, the store, the content, the leads, the support are all *capabilities underneath* — proof points a visitor discovers after the hook lands, never the headline itself.

For the first lead-collection surface (the waitlist gate), keep the current waitlist's structure but tighten it around this one angle. The current page is already 90% there: "Your **business**, run by an AI agent" is the exact right headline. Hold that line as the single best hook across every surface — landing hero, waitlist, email, ad, ProductHunt, Telegram welcome.

## 2. RANKED CANDIDATE ANGLES (pick from these)

### #1 — THE LIVING BUSINESS AGENT (recommended winner)
- **Core promise (one sentence):** Hire one agent that runs your whole business from Telegram — it handles the work, thinks ahead, and you approve everything.
- **Strongest supporting copy (quoted):** From `seo-assets/waitlist.html`: *"GodsEye is a living agent that sits inside your Telegram and handles your whole office. It books clients, replies to messages, posts your content, tracks orders, chases reminders, and keeps everything moving. You tell it once. It never forgets."* — plus its perk line: *"Show it a task one time. It learns, and from then on it just does it, on schedule, without you asking twice."*
- **Who it appeals to:** The widest net. Solopreneurs, service pros (beauty, tutors, consultants), store owners, creators, small businesses, non-technical owners — anyone who runs anything. Not limited to WordPress users.

### #2 — THE PROACTIVE PARTNER ("It thinks ahead")
- **Core promise (one sentence):** Your agent doesn't wait to be told — it watches your business around the clock and brings useful work to you.
- **Strongest supporting copy (quoted):** From `drafts/HERO-10-FINAL.md` (Option 8): *"Your agent doesn't wait for instructions. It actively works — monitoring your business, finding improvements, sending you nudges and suggestions. 'Should I post this?' 'This client hasn't replied in 3 days — follow up?' 'Your top product is trending — want a promo?' You approve. It's done."*
- **Who it appeals to:** Busy/overwhelmed owners who want the load *carried* for them, and skeptical buyers who need to see it's not just another chatbot. This is the differentiator against ChatGPT ("ChatGPT talks. Godseye does.").

### #3 — THE ONE-MAN ARMY / BACKUP TEAM ("You're doing the work of five people")
- **Core promise (one sentence):** Fill every seat of your business with agents that do the work of a whole team, for less than one part-time hire.
- **Strongest supporting copy (quoted):** From `drafts/HERO-15-OPTIONS.md` (Option 11): *"You're doing the work of five people. Stop. Your agent takes over the roles you're drowning in — the customer replies, the content, the scheduling, the follow-ups, the admin work. It's your clerk, your receptionist, your social media manager, your support agent — all in one, all the time, for less than you'd pay one part-timer."*
- **Who it appeals to:** Solopreneurs, lone wolves, one-person teams, freelancers and studio owners who resent doing the back-office work. Also a strong "look bigger than you are" status angle (PSYCHOLOGY Option 4).

### #4 — SAFETY & CONTROL ("You're always the boss")
- **Core promise (one sentence):** The agent you can trust with your real business, because it only touches what you approve and you can cut access in a word.
- **Strongest supporting copy (quoted):** From `seo-assets/waitlist.html` perk: *"You stay in control. It only touches what you let it. Everything ships through you. Cut its access any time, in a word."* — reinforced by `FULL-LANDING-PAGE-COPY.md` closing line: *"You approve. It's done. That's what it looks like when an agent grows with your business."*
- **Who it appeals to:** Trust-anxious buyers, especially non-technical owners who fear an AI breaking their site or getting out of hand. This is the objection-killer, best used as support near the CTA rather than the headline.

### #5 — SIMPLE, PLAIN, NON-TECHNICAL ("It's just a chat" / the Vicky angle)
- **Core promise (one sentence):** If you can send a text message, you can run your business with Godseye — no website, no computer, no learning curve.
- **Strongest supporting copy (quoted):** From `drafts/HERO-10-PSYCHOLOGY.md` (Option 7): *"It's just another chat on your phone. That runs your entire business. You already know how to use Telegram. You already chat with people every day. This is the same thing — except the person on the other end is your AI agent."*
- **Who it appeals to:** The absolute normie — lash techs, hairstylists, shop owners, anyone who "sells, not codes" (the Vicky test in `HERO-COPY-DRAFT-v3-VICKY.md`). The lowest-friction pitch, but too *thin* alone; use the *energy* of it as the tone, not the whole argument.

## 3. THE MASTER NARRATIVE (one seamless flow)

This is the single most cohesive message sequence, weaving the best bits from every draft. Use it as the spine for the landing hero slider, the waitlist page, the email sequence, and the first Telegram message.

### HOOK (understand + feel)
> *It's just a chat on your phone. That runs your entire business.*
> You already live in Telegram. So does your agent.

### PROBLEM (relief — name the pain honestly)
> *You're tired of doing everything yourself. The replies. The follow-ups. The content. The orders. The admin. The stuff that falls through the cracks. You're doing the work of five people.*

### SOLUTION (the entity, plain language)
> *Hire one agent. It lives where you already are, and it handles the things you'd otherwise stress over. Tell it once. It learns. It never forgets. No website required. No computer required. No serious learning curve.*

### PROOF (the differentiator — proactive + safe)
> *And it doesn't just answer. It thinks ahead. "Your top product is trending — want a promo?" "A client hasn't been back in three weeks — send a follow-up?" "Your store's slow — I can fix it now." You approve. It's done.*
> *Everything ships through you. Cut its access any time, in a word. You're always the boss.*

### CTA (cheap, no barrier, one action)
> *Hire your agent from $0.30 a day. No credit card to start. Open Telegram. Your agent is waiting.*

**Why this sequence works:** It mirrors the PSYCHOLOGY draft's mapped journey (understand → feel → justify → discover depth → belong), and it follows the CONVERSION-BLUEPRINT's core idea that "I have too much business work → Godseye takes some off my plate → I can try it cheaply → I open Telegram and begin." Every ingredient — the living-agent framing, the self-autonomy, the proactive nudges, the safety, the low price, the non-technical tone — is in the recommended angle, presented in the order a skeptic needs it.

## 4. CONFLICT / CONSOLIDATION NOTES (founder must decide)

### 4.1 WordPress-focus vs whole-business focus — THE big one
- **Old / live-adjacent framing** (PRD §1, brand-voice `pillars`, marketing-fleet README, Content Brief, ProductHunt kit, waitlist-nurture emails): Godseye = *AI WordPress management agent* — "Your WordPress site, handled. Just talk to it on Telegram." Everything resolves around the website.
- **New / drafted framing** (CONVERSION-BLUEPRINT, PRODUCT-STANDARD §1, HERO v2/v3, HERO-15, FULL-LANDING-PAGE-COPY, DOMAIN-FIRST-ONBOARDING): Godseye = *a whole-business agent* — "one agent you hire to run work for your business, from Telegram." WordPress is "the first concrete capability... not the whole identity."
- **Recommendation:** These are not mutually exclusive. **Lead with whole-business, treat WordPress as proof/use-case.** PRODUCT-STANDARD §1 says it best: *"WordPress is the first concrete capability and the first activation path. It is not the whole identity of the product."* The ProductHunt/SEO audience may still convert better on the WordPress keyword — keep it as a landing-page *path* and blog category, never the H1.

### 4.2 Pricing model — lived vs approved (NOT settled, this is a real mismatch)
- **Lived on the site today** (`mockData.ts` `PRICING_PLANS`/`CREDIT_PACKS` + PRD §4): **credits** — Free $0/50 credits, Starter $9/mo (500 credits), Pro $29 (2,000), Agency $99 (10,000); plus one-time credit packs; plus founder 50%-off half-pricing ($4.50/$14.50/$49.50).
- **Approved-but-unreleased draft** (`PRICING-DRAFT.ts`, CONVERSION-BLUEPRINT §3, FULL-LANDING-PAGE-COPY §9, FULL-SITE-RESTRUCTURE): **hours + hires** — 1h trial $9, 10h $69, 50h $249, 100h $399; monthly Starter $9 / Pro $29 / VPS $99; "from $0.30/day"; add-ons (extra hours, site migration $49, agent packs $29, domain setup $19).
- **Marketing-fleet README** adds a *third* variant: Starter $9 (was $29), Pro $29 (was $49), God Mode $99 (was $149) — discount framing that disagrees with PRD's "50% off founder" and with EMAIL_COPY's reward-as-credits (not discount) decision.
- **Recommendation (for copy only):** The drafts converge on **hours + hires, "from $0.30 a day," "cancel anytime," "no credit card to start."** Do not publish a specific price until the founder confirms which model is real and the store matches the site. The PDF/commentary and emails should avoid promising "credits" (old) or a specific discount % (conflicting) until resolved. **This is the single biggest conversion leak** per BUILD-EXEC-PACK-READY — the landing sells an agent, but `/start` and `/pricing` still sell WordPress credits.

### 4.3 Tone / brand-voice disagreement between docs
- **Two brand voices exist.** (a) The warm, plain, moral "Architect/caretaker, human-likable, calm, no hype" voice (brand-voice.md, PRODUCT-STANDARD §6/§9, EMAIL_COPY: "confident, direct, ship fast... no overpromising"). (b) More emotive/greedy psych-driven hooks (PSYCHOLOGY: "You'd be stupid not to," "Greed/Laziness/Fear" triggers). 
- **Also inconsistent:** PRD tagline *"Your Personal AI Developer, 24/7"* vs later *"business agent"* framing; marketing-fleet README says *"communicate... never 'text'"* while OTHER drafts (HERO-10, PSYCHOLOGY) repeatedly use "you just talk to it," "text me," "open a chat."
- **HARD RULE from brand-voice + PRODUCT-STANDARD §9:** say **agent / work / domain / workspace / subscription**, say **"talk to your agent on Telegram" not "text an AI."** Avoid "AI" as a buzzword; "entity" and "agent" preferred. The founder values **plain, human language, no em-dashes, no jargon** — several drafts still contain em-dashes and jargon-y "steer from Telegram" (explicitly removed in later drafts). **Recommendation:** adopt the calm, plain, human voice as the single voice; keep the hunger of the psych hooks in the *pacing and sequence*, not in sleazy lines.

### 4.4 Headline / hero disagreement
- **The waitlist lives on** *"Your business, run by an AI agent"* — the strongest single hook.
- Later hero drafts favor *"Hire an AI agent / talk to it / your business by simply talking"* and role-based (*"Your store manager. Your content writer. Your analyst."*), and explicitly REMOVE "steer from Telegram," "company on autopilot," and "Beautiful solopreneurs" (typo) phrasing.
- **Recommendation:** Keep "Your business, run by an AI agent" as the **hero headline**. Use a simple **subline** (e.g., "Tell it once. It handles the rest. You keep the approvals.") rather than a second competing H1. Role-based and fleet content is strong as *mid-page proof*, not the headline.

### 4.5 "Free" positioning — preview vs full product
- `FULL-LANDING-PAGE-COPY` and `PRICING-DRAFT` both propose a **free/30-min trial** and a "plug it in" form replacing the waitlist.
- `PRODUCT-STANDARD` (§2, §5) insists the free tier is a **preview workspace** that must NOT promise live domain work without a subscription: *"No active subscription, no real-domain connection and no live business execution."*
- **Recommendation for copy:** Never claim a free user can publish/manage a real live site (that breaks trust and the licensing model). Say the free step *shows* the business space and produces a sample result; live work is unlocked by the subscription. This matches both trust-safety and the honesty rule.

### 4.6 Domain-first (broader) vs Telegram-only
- `DOMAIN-FIRST-ONBOARDING` argues Godseye should help put a business **online** (domain, site, web presence) as the first visible surface — *"You tell it what the business is. It helps you put it online, then keeps improving it."*
- Other drafts (CONVERSION-BLUEPRINT, v3-VICKY) strongly emphasize *"No website required... it works from Telegram alone"* — because Vicky has no site.
- **Recommendation for copy:** Don't lock either. The safest all-around line: *"Your agent works from Telegram alone. Got a website or store? It can manage that too."* This satisfies both the no-website normie and the site-owner. Let the *onboarding* branch by "do you have a website," not the headline.

### 4.7 Proactive/autonomy claim degree
- Some drafts push "fully autonomous, it just runs, you sleep." PRODUCT-STANDARD explicitly forbids promising "fully autonomous" or "never makes mistakes" without guardrails: *"No unsupported 'unlimited,' 'never makes mistakes,' or 'fully autonomous' claims."* People, controls, and approval stay. **Recommendation:** "It thinks ahead / it doesn't wait for instructions" is safe and differentiating; avoid "it runs itself with zero supervision" as an absolute claim.
